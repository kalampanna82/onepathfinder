Common questions

Are you a law firm?

No. One Path Finder is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice. Immigration document preparation is offered only where permitted by law.

Can you guarantee an approval or a refund?

No. We can't guarantee any outcome. Decisions are made by the government agency that reviews your application or tax return.

Where can I get official forms?

Official forms and instructions are free from the agencies. USCIS forms are at uscis.gov/forms, and IRS forms are at irs.gov/forms-instructions.

Who can answer legal questions about my case?

A licensed attorney or a DOJ-accredited representative can advise you on your specific situation.
