How much is the SEVIS fee?

The SEVIS I-901 fee is $350 for F and M students and $220 for most J-1 exchange visitors. Choose your visa type and situation to see what you pay.

What the SEVIS fee is

SEVIS is the Student and Exchange Visitor Information System, run by U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE). The I-901 fee pays for running it. You pay it once for your Form I-20 or DS-2019, and your school or program sponsor does not pay it for you unless they choose to.

When to pay

  • Outside the U.S. and you need a visa: pay before your visa interview. You can book the interview first, but allow at least three business days for the payment to be verified.
  • Outside the U.S. and you do not need a visa: pay and allow processing time before you arrive. You cannot pay at the port of entry.
  • Inside the U.S.: pay before you apply for a change of status or reinstatement, if a fee applies to your situation.

Good to know

  • There are no fee waivers. A school, sponsor or family member can pay for you.
  • Refunds are limited. Duplicate payments and payments made when no fee was required can be refunded, and you must ask within 90 days of the payment. Fees are not refunded if your visa is denied or you decide not to come.
  • The SEVIS fee is not the visa application fee. You pay the visa fee separately to the U.S. embassy or consulate. See the State Department fee page.
  • This tool covers the most common cases. Rules for continuing students and exchange visitors have more exceptions, so read the ICE I-901 questions and answers if yours is unusual.
