OPT premium processing day tracker

USCIS has 30 business days to act on a premium processing request for F-1 OPT or STEM OPT. Business days leave out weekends and federal holidays, so 30 business days is about six calendar weeks. Enter your date to see where you are.

An estimate, not USCIS’s count. This tool counts from a calendar. USCIS decides the real start date and which days count. Check your case status on the USCIS website. This is general information, not legal advice.

How the 30 business days work

  • When it starts. The count begins when USCIS receives your Form I-907 and the premium processing fee. Receipt day is day 0, and business day 1 is the next business day.
  • What counts as a business day. Monday to Friday, except federal holidays. When a holiday falls on a weekend, the Friday or Monday it is observed is skipped. This tool skips the 11 federal holidays.
  • What “acting” means. USCIS can approve, deny, or send a request for evidence or a notice of intent to deny within the period. An approval is not guaranteed.
  • The card is separate. After approval, producing and mailing the EAD card can take another one to three weeks. That time is not part of the 30 business days.
  • Premium processing fee. $1,780 for I-765 OPT and STEM OPT, paid on top of the I-765 filing fee. See the USCIS fee calculator.

If 30 business days pass

  • Check your case status online first. The notice may be in the mail.
  • USCIS says it refunds the premium processing fee when it does not act in time, and it keeps working on the case. Ask how to request it through your USCIS account or the Contact Center (800-375-5283).
  • Talk to your school’s international student office (your DSO) before you change travel or work plans.
