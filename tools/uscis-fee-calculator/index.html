USCIS filing fee calculator

Add up the government filing fees for common immigration forms. Choose the forms you plan to file.

Confirm before you pay. Fees change, and a wrong fee can cause USCIS to reject your filing. These amounts were last checked in October 2026. Confirm the exact fee on the official USCIS fee calculator before you file. This is general information, not legal advice. It covers government fees only, not attorney or service fees.

Check each form you plan to file. Then choose online or paper.

What is included

Biometric services are included in these fees. This tool does not cover every form, every fee category, fee waivers (except the reduced N-400 fee shown), or fees for asylum, parole, temporary protected status and similar filings. Some applicants pay a different fee or $0, such as certain military applicants. Check the official fee schedule for your situation.

USCIS has proposed raising the N-400 fee. A proposal is not final, so this tool uses the current fee. Check the official sources for updates before you file.

Privacy: Everything is calculated in your browser. What you enter is not sent to us or stored.
