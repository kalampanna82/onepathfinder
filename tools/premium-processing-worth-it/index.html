Questions to ask yourself

  • Do I have a hard date? If nothing depends on the timing, the money may not buy you much.
  • Is my case ready? Missing documents can bring a request for evidence either way, and that restarts the clock. The fee does not fix a weak case.
  • Who pays? For many work visas the employer files and pays for premium processing. Ask before you pay.
  • Is regular processing already fast? If it is close to the premium deadline, you save little.
  • What happens after approval? A work permit card can take one to three more weeks to be made and mailed, with or without premium processing.

What the fee does not change

  • It does not make approval more likely.
  • It does not make a green card available sooner. Your priority date still has to be current in the Visa Bulletin.
  • It does not speed up other steps, such as a visa interview or a background check.
